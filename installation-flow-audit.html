The flow

From booking to CSP response

A verified booking becomes a connection, the system assigns the best CSP and creates a task. The CSP is notified — a push notification (PN) and a full-page notification (FPN) go to the Manager, Manager+ and Owner. The CSP must then respond: propose a slot (happy case) or decline (instant reassignment). If they do neither within 2 working hours, the booking is pulled back and given to another CSP — the customer waits that entire time.
